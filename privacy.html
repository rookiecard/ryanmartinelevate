Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026 · Effective: June 10, 2026

Your privacy and discretion are central to how Elevate operates. This policy describes the limited information we collect and how we protect it.

Elevate by Ryan Martin ("Elevate," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Mother Nature Knows Best FZCO, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through www.elevate-coaching.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. Because Elevate operates on an application-only basis, the information we hold is limited and handled with discretion.

01 Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit an application or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, professional background, and any information you choose to share about your health, performance goals, or circumstances. You provide this voluntarily.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our Site may automatically collect limited technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages viewed. This is used for security and to understand how the Site is used.

Sensitive information. Coaching applications may involve health-related details. We treat any health, lifestyle, or personal information you share as confidential and use it only to assess fit and deliver our services. We do not sell this information under any circumstances.

02 How We Use Your Information

  • To review and respond to your application
  • To communicate with you about a potential or active engagement
  • To deliver, personalize, and improve our coaching services
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Site
  • To comply with legal obligations

03 How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, email, scheduling, payment processing, or form hosting), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Legal authorities where required by law or to protect rights and safety

04 Cookies & Analytics

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and measure usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may not function as intended if you do. If we use a third-party analytics provider, it processes data on our behalf under its own terms.

05 Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here, or as required by law. If your application is not accepted and there is no ongoing relationship, you may request deletion of your information at any time.

06 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@elevate-coaching.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

07 Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take the confidentiality of our clientele seriously.

08 International Visitors

Elevate serves clients worldwide and is operated from the United Arab Emirates. By using the Site, you understand your information may be processed in jurisdictions with different data-protection laws than your own.

09 Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@elevate-coaching.com.
